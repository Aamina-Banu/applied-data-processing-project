The January 2025 dataset contains seven Status values: Closed (335,732), In Progress (845), Open (425), Assigned (274), Pending (163), Started (76), and Unspecified (36). Closed is treated as the closed-status category; In Progress, Open, Assigned, Pending, and Started are treated as non-closed statuses; Unspecified is kept separate because its meaning is uncertain. Status and Closed Date will be considered together when classifying records.

A cross-check between Status and Closed Date found that 334,707 records have Closed status with a present Closed Date. There are also conflicting combinations: 1,025 Closed records have no Closed Date, while 267 records with non-closed statuses have a Closed Date. The 36 Unspecified records are kept separate because their status is uncertain. These groups will be reported separately rather than silently reclassified.
